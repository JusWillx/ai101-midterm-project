ABOUT THE PROJECT

BUILT FOR CAR PEOPLE.

VRT Garage is a student-built virtual automotive customization concept for enthusiasts who want to explore their dream builds before spending money on real modifications.

Stage 2 adds a three-car showroom and a matched front/rear photo gallery for the featured Shelby GT500. Future stages will add paint and wheel options, photo uploads, and potentially AI-powered previews.
